# Contributing

Thank you for helping. System Spec gets better when teams who need a whole-system specification bring what they have
already built.

- **Questions and ideas:** open an issue.
- **A change to a layer's format, or a new layer:** open a pull request with a decision record in `docs/decisions/`
  using the MADR 4 template, following [docs/extending.md](docs/extending.md).
- **Tools:** corrections to [docs/tooling.md](docs/tooling.md), especially entries marked *unverified*, and new
  validators, renderers or exporters (to Spec Kit, OpenSpec, Backstage).
- **Examples:** a real bundle, even a partial one, is the most useful contribution of all.

Contributions are under the [MIT licence](LICENSE).
