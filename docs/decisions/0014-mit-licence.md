# License the standard under MIT

## Context and Problem Statement

Anyone should be able to copy the templates and schemas into their own projects and tools.

## Considered Options

* MIT
* MIT OR CC0-1.0, as MADR uses
* Apache-2.0

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: "MIT", because it is the most familiar permissive licence and the maintainers chose it.

### Consequences

* Templates copied into a bundle carry no obligations beyond the notice in this repository

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### MIT

* Good, because it is short, permissive and familiar

### MIT OR CC0-1.0, as MADR uses

* Good, because CC0 suits text
* Bad, because a dual licence is one more thing to explain

### Apache-2.0

* Good, because of its patent grant
* Bad, because longer
