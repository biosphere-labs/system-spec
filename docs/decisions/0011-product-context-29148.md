# Follow ISO 29148's stakeholder-requirements outline for product context

## Context and Problem Statement

Agent software factories research the product category, users and comparable products before planning.

## Decision Drivers

* Coverage: the most that can be expressed precisely
* An existing open standard where one exists
* Written by LLMs with skills and reviewed by people, so ease of learning does not count
* Turns into countable, testable units

## Considered Options

* ISO 29148 outline plus research fields
* Lean Canvas
* Product Vision Board

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: "ISO 29148 outline plus research fields", because it is the most complete recognised structure.

### Consequences

* We follow its structure without copying its text

### Confirmation

The readiness rules in [SPEC.md](../SPEC.md) section 6 check this layer.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### ISO 29148 outline plus research fields

* Good, because it is the most complete recognised outline
* Bad, because the standard is paywalled

### Lean Canvas

* Bad, because it has startup fields we do not need

### Product Vision Board

* Bad, because it is not machine-readable
